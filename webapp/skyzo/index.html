Skyzo (English)

A calm card game for Android: 12 cards, few points, clever moves.

  • Play against 1 to 7 computer opponents at three difficulty levels.
  • Three equal cards in a column disappear – the lowest score at the end wins.
  • Guided tutorial, rule variants, light and dark mode, German and English.
  • Fully offline, no ads, no account, no data collection.
